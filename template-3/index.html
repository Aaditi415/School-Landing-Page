11 — GOOD QUESTIONS

Wondering
about something?

Here are a few helpful starting points. If you can't find what you need, just ask us.

Ask our team
How do I apply to Aurelia University?

Start by exploring your program, then contact the admissions team for current eligibility, application steps and deadlines.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Campus visits can help you understand the learning spaces and student environment. Contact the team to ask about available visit options.

Are scholarships or financial support available?

Scholarship availability, eligibility and deadlines vary. Please contact admissions for the latest verified information.

What student clubs and activities are offered?

Student communities may include arts, technology, culture, sports, volunteering and entrepreneurship. Ask the student team for the current list.

Where can I find course fees and entry requirements?

Fees and entry requirements depend on the program and intake. Request the current prospectus from the admissions team.
