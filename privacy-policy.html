Privacy Policy.

01

Introduction

Aurelia International School ("Aurelia", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy of visitors, parents, guardians and other individuals who interact with our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you visit our website, use its features or contact our school.

For this policy, "website" means the official Aurelia International School website and its pages and interactive features.

02

Information We Collect

The information involved depends on how you interact with the website.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact the school or submit an enquiry through a functioning enquiry channel, you may be asked to provide information such as:

  • Parent or guardian name.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Subject of your enquiry.
  • Grade or class you are enquiring about.
  • Message or other details you choose to share.

Technical information

Our website hosting provider or technical service providers may process standard technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information and server access logs, as part of delivering and securing the website. The actual information collected depends on the hosting configuration.

Please avoid sharing sensitive information

Do not send passwords, financial account details, identity documents, medical records or unnecessary sensitive information through general website enquiries or the chatbot.

03

How We Use Information

Where information is actually collected by the school, it may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to parent and guardian enquiries.
  • Providing information about admissions.
  • Communicating about campus visits.
  • Responding to questions about school programmes and facilities.
  • Maintaining website safety and functionality.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

We aim to use personal information only for appropriate purposes and in accordance with applicable requirements.

04

Chatbot & Contact Form

Aurelia Assistant

The current Aurelia Assistant is a JavaScript-based chatbot. It uses programmed question patterns and predefined school information to generate responses.

In its current implementation, chat messages are handled in the visitor's browser session. The chatbot does not connect to an AI service or send chat messages to a school server. Messages are not saved by the chatbot after the page session ends.

Website enquiry form

The current website enquiry form performs browser-side validation and displays a demonstration confirmation. It does not currently transmit enquiry details to the school or submit them to a backend service.

If a real enquiry service, email integration, chatbot provider or backend is added later, this policy must be updated to explain what information is transmitted, who processes it and how long it is retained.

05

Cookies & Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party resources to display fonts, icons or other website features. The current design uses Google Fonts and a Lucide icon library delivered through external content delivery services.

When your browser requests these resources, the relevant third-party providers may receive technical information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies govern their processing of that information.

The current website code does not intentionally install advertising or analytics tracking cookies. This should be checked again if analytics, embedded media, maps or other third-party integrations are introduced.

06

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

If the school collects personal information through future enquiry or admissions services, it may be shared with authorised personnel or service providers where necessary to respond to requests, operate school services or meet legal obligations.

Any such sharing should be limited to appropriate purposes and handled in accordance with applicable law.

07

Data Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect information handled by the school.

However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should avoid sharing confidential information through unsecured communication channels.

08

Children's Privacy

Aurelia is a school website and may be visited by children as well as adults. General website enquiries should be made by parents or legal guardians.

We do not intend for the general website chatbot or enquiry form to collect sensitive personal information directly from children. Any future collection of children's personal information for school-related purposes must follow applicable consent, safeguarding and legal requirements.

09

Your Choices & Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances in which information is processed, you may have rights to request access, correction, erasure or other actions relating to your personal information.

To make a privacy-related request, contact the school using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, school practices or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
